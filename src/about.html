About TaftEats

Helping students eat better, one review at a time

👥

Who We Are

TaftEats is a student-centered food discovery platform designed to help students and food lovers find honest, reliable restaurant recommendations around Taft Avenue. Built with accessibility and authenticity in mind, we prioritize real experiences over hype.

🎯

Our Mission

Our mission is to support informed dining decisions by providing a space where students can share genuine feedback. TaftEats aims to uplift local food establishments while helping users make choices that fit their taste, budget, and lifestyle.

🍽️

What We Offer

  • Curated restaurants around Taft
  • Student-written reviews and ratings
  • Search and filter tools for convenience
  • A platform built specifically for students
